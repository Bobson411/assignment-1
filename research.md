# Research


My goals are similar to the ideas discussed in Susan F. Belgrad's chapter, "Portfolios and E-Portfolios: Student Reflection, Self-Assessment, and Goal Setting in the Learning Process." Belgrad explains that portfolios are meant to show a student's progress, effort, and their achievements they gained over time and that they encourage self-reflection, assessment and goal setting. My professional, recreational, lifestyle, and personal growth goals follow a similar idea because they make me think about where I am now and what I want to accomplish in the future. But, my goals are broader than the academic goals discussed in the chapter because they include parts of my life outside of school and my career, such as travel, hobbies, and independence. My goals focus on reflecting on my progress and using that to plan for my future. 

## Reference

Belgrad, S. (2013). Portfolios and E-Portfolios: Student Reflection, Self-Assessment, and Goal Setting in the Learning Process. In *SAGE Handbook of Research on Classroom Assessment* (pp. 331–346).
